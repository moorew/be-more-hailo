Samples the artwork paths below into morph-compatible contours (same point count, top edge then bottom edge) and builds shapes.json. To add or redraw a mouth or eye, paste its SVG path into MOUTHS / EYES in this file, open it in a browser, and copy the downloaded file to bmo_face/ and static/face/. Critter art comes from critters_src.js (the SVGs in svg_faces/).
